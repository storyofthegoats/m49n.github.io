01 / The profile

Two sides of
the same table.

An operations leader who became an auditor—and brought the perspective of the operator along.

My career has moved from leading senior living communities and multi-state operations to evaluating the financial reporting processes, technology controls, and governance structures behind them. That combination shapes how I approach risk: with an understanding of both what a control is supposed to accomplish and what it takes to make it work in practice.
