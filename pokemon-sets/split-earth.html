Split Earth

裂けた大地

English counterpart: Skyridge

Split Earth combines a strong Eeveelution lineup with three very different Crystal chases. Ho-Oh supplies the legendary centerpiece, while Crystal Golem and Kabutops give the set a distinctly ancient flavor. Umbreon and the original Eevee evolutions make the regular holo section worth exploring as well.

Chase cards: Crystal Ho-Oh 091/088, Crystal Kabutops 090/088, Crystal Golem 089/088, and holo Umbreon 072/088.

Set fact: Split Earth and Mysterious Mountains became the English Skyridge expansion. Their Crystal trios are different: Ho-Oh, Kabutops, and Golem belong here.
