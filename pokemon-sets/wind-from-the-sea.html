Wind from the Sea

海からの風

English counterpart: Aquapolis

Wind from the Sea is where the Japanese Crystal hunt begins. Crystal Lugia is the natural centerpiece, with Kingdra and Nidoking completing the secret trio. The regular holos still deserve attention: Tyranitar and Entei give collectors good reasons to explore the checklist beyond those final three cards.

Chase cards: Crystal Lugia 090/087, Crystal Kingdra 089/087, Crystal Nidoking 088/087, and holo Tyranitar 071/087.

Set fact: Crystal Pokémon have a Crystal Type Poké-Body that changes their type in response to attached Energy. Here, all three are numbered above the printed 87-card denominator.
