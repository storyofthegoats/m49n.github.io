1997 · Original era

Rocket Gang

ロケット団

English counterpart: Team Rocket

Rocket Gang gives familiar Pokémon a more mischievous identity. The Dark evolutions make the set feel like a collection of alternate personalities, with Dark Charizard, Dark Blastoise, and Dark Dragonite carrying much of its collector appeal. The Trainer cards help tell the story too: Team Rocket is present throughout the set, not just in its name.

Chase cards: Dark Charizard, Dark Blastoise, Dark Dragonite, and Dark Alakazam holos.

Set fact: This expansion introduced Dark Pokémon. It also gives you holo cards beyond the Pokémon themselves, including Here Comes Team Rocket! and Rainbow Energy.
