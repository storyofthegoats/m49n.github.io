2004 · PCG era

Clash of the Blue Sky

蒼空の激突

English counterpart: EX Deoxys

Clash of the Blue Sky brings Rayquaza and Deoxys into the spotlight, with Gold Star Rayquaza as the standout collecting target. Gold Star Latias and Latios complete the trio, while Salamence ex and the two numbered Deoxys ex cards give the set plenty to chase below that top tier. Its regular holo lineup also includes Gyarados, Metagross, Jirachi, and Crawdaunt.

Chase cards: Gold Star Rayquaza 067/082, Latias 065/082, Latios 066/082, and Salamence ex 064/082.

Set fact: The three Gold Stars sit inside the regular 82-card numbering. A Gold Star does not automatically have an over-the-limit number like the Crystal secret cards in the earlier e-Card sets.
