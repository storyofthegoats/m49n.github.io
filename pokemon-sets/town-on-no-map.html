The Town on No Map

地図にない町

English counterpart: Aquapolis

The Town on No Map is an Eeveelution highlight of the Japanese e-Card era. Umbreon and Espeon anchor the hunt, with Suicune and Arcanine rounding out an especially attractive holo lineup. Its wide e-Card borders and separately numbered holo versions make it a rewarding set to learn by card number.

Chase cards: Umbreon 068/092, Espeon 046/092, Suicune 031/092, and Arcanine 017/092—all holos.

Set fact: This is one of the two Japanese expansions combined into Aquapolis. Aquapolis’s Crystal Pokémon come from its partner, Wind from the Sea; The Town on No Map has no Crystal cards.
